01 / ABOUT

Meet Headboy.

Headboy is a Nigerian Afrobeats artist whose music blends contemporary African sounds with a distinctive, energetic style.

Known professionally as Headboy, Abhulimen Itohan Daniel has released music across major digital platforms and collaborated with artists and producers across Africa. His catalogue includes collaborations with Asake, Medikal and Rekles, among others.

This is the official online home for Headboy's music, releases, videos, collaborations and media information.
