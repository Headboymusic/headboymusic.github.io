01 / ABOUT

Meet Headboy.

Headboy (Abhulimen Itohan Daniel) is a Nigerian Afrobeats artist known for a distinctive contemporary African sound.

Professionally known as Headboy, Abhulimen Itohan Daniel has released music across major digital platforms and collaborated with artists and producers across Africa. His catalogue includes Lagos Boy featuring Asake, Again featuring Medikal, Naughty Girl featuring Rekles and Get Up featuring Mohbad.

This is the official online home for Headboy's music, releases, videos, collaborations and media information.
